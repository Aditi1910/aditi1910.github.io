// 01 · About

Finance background.
Analytics mind.
Storyteller at heart.

I turn messy, multi-source data into the answers that decide what a business, a product, or a campaign does next. Nearly four years in financial services plus an MS in Business Analytics gave me the mix most teams are missing: analytical depth and real domain fluency.

My work lives where data, statistics, and strategy meet. I don't stop at the dashboard. I dig into why a number moved, what it signals for the coming quarter, and what a team should actually do with it.

From credit risk models across 50+ accounts to a profitability diagnostic that found where a $2.3M business was leaking margin, every project starts from the same place: what should someone do differently because of this?

That range is deliberate. Different industries, one throughline: get to the insight that changes what happens next.

Make It or Buy It?

Face cream costs $32.15 a carton to make and $40 to buy — and the optimum still buys all 12,000. Hand cream can’t be outsourced, and it swallows 7,200 of the 7,500 lb of scent, leaving nothing to make anything else with. Sensitivity prices that scent at $50.96 a pound against a $3 purchase price, which says buy more scent before you hand a competitor your formulas.

Case study · Transshipment LP

Which Airport Should It Fly From?

Three South Indian warehouses, one Frankfurt run, and airline rates that shift by city and by weekday. Twelve route variables solved per shipping day find Rs 47,600 a week — but Rs 42,000 of it lands on Saturday alone. Tuesday’s Rs 5,600 doesn’t cover a 2% chance of a penalty equal to the freight, so the recommendation is to truck selectively rather than wherever the model says.
